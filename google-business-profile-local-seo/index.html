Google Business Profile: the local SEO job most small businesses leave half-done

For anyone serving a local area, this free listing often matters more than the website.

SmallBizWire Editors · 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

When someone searches "plumber near me" or "cafe open now", the map results usually appear before any website. Those results come from Google Business Profile. It is free, and most profiles are incomplete.

Get the basics exactly right

  • Name: your real business name. Adding keywords to the name breaches Google's guidelines and can get a listing suspended.
  • Category: the primary category matters a great deal. Choose the most specific accurate one, then add secondary categories.
  • Address or service area: show an address only if customers visit you there.
  • Hours, including holiday hours. Wrong hours generate bad reviews quickly.
  • Phone and website, matching what is on your site.

Fill in what competitors skip

  • Services or products with short descriptions and prices where sensible.
  • Real photos: the outside (so people can find you), the inside, the team, the work.
  • Attributes such as accessibility and payment options.
  • The business description, written for people rather than keywords.

Reviews

Ask every satisfied customer, with a direct link to your review form. Reply to every review, good or bad, briefly and professionally. Do not offer incentives for reviews and never post fake ones; both break Google's policies, and fake reviews are increasingly targeted by consumer-protection regulators.

Keep it alive

Post updates, offers or events occasionally, answer the Questions section, and update photos seasonally. An active profile signals a business that is open and cared for.

Connect it to your website

Make sure your name, address and phone number appear identically on your website, and add LocalBusiness structured data. Consistency across the web helps Google trust the details. For broader visibility, including in AI assistants' answers, see our guide to AI search.

Questions readers ask

Is Google Business Profile free?

Yes. Creating and managing a profile costs nothing.

Can I add keywords to my business name on Google?

No. Google's guidelines require your real business name, and keyword stuffing can lead to suspension.
