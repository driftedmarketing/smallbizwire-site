About SmallBizWire

Who publishes this site, and how we handle our own products.

SmallBizWire is published by Drifted Marketing. Drifted Marketing is a marketing agency (driftedmarketing.com) and also makes Housefly, a texted AI assistant (heyhousefly.com).

Why we say this up front

Some articles on this site mention Drifted Marketing or Housefly, including in lists alongside other agencies and tools. Because we own them, we have a direct interest. Every page that features either one carries an inline disclosure, and every page carries the publisher line in its footer.

Editorial rules

  • No invented quotes, reviews, awards, testimonials or client results.
  • No staff personas. Articles are written by the SmallBizWire editors, an in-house team at Drifted Marketing.
  • Statistics appear only when they come from a public, checkable source, such as a mail provider's published sender rules.
  • Lists that include our own products also include well-known alternatives, described fairly, with notes on who each suits.
  • No paid placements. Nobody pays to appear on SmallBizWire.

Corrections

If something here is wrong or out of date, tell us through driftedmarketing.com and we will fix it and note the change.
