How to write a Shopify product page that ranks and sells

A product page has two readers: a search engine deciding what it is about, and a buyer deciding whether to trust you.

SmallBizWire Editors · 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

The same page has to satisfy two audiences. Fortunately, what helps one usually helps the other: clarity, specifics and honest detail.

The title

Lead with what the item is, then the distinguishing detail: material, size, colour or model. "Hand-embroidered cotton cushion cover, 18 inch, sage" tells both a shopper and a search engine exactly what is on offer. Keep brand names and clever product nicknames secondary.

The description

  • Open with the benefit in one or two sentences: what problem it solves or what it is for.
  • Follow with specifics in a scannable list: dimensions, materials, care, what is in the box.
  • Answer objections: delivery times, returns, sizing advice.
  • Write it yourself. Manufacturer copy is duplicated across dozens of sites and gives search engines no reason to prefer yours.

Photographs

Show scale, texture and the product in use. Add descriptive alt text to each image. Compress files so the page loads quickly on a phone, which is where most shoppers will see it.

Variants

Where colours or sizes are variants of one product, keep them on one page rather than creating near-identical listings that compete with each other.

Reviews

Genuine reviews help shoppers and can appear in search results through structured data. Never write or buy reviews; it breaches platform and search policies and, in many countries, consumer law. Ask real customers after delivery instead.

The search listing

Edit the SEO title and meta description in Shopify's "Search engine listing" panel. Use the description to state the reason to click: free delivery threshold, handmade, ships in two days, whatever is true and distinctive.

Internal links

Link from the product to its collection and to one or two related items. Link to the product from relevant blog posts and guides. Pages that nothing links to are harder for search engines to find and judge.
