How to write a Shopify product page that ranks and sells

A product page has two readers: a search engine deciding what it is about, and a buyer deciding whether to trust you.

SmallBizWire Editorial Team · 30 October 2026 · 4 min read

Short answer: A Shopify product page that ranks and sells has a clear, searchable product title, a unique SEO title and meta description, an original description that answers buyers' real questions, compressed photos with descriptive alt text, visible price, shipping and returns information, genuine reviews, and structured data so search engines can show price and availability.

The same page has to satisfy two audiences. Fortunately, what helps one usually helps the other: clarity, specifics and honest detail.

The title

Lead with what the item is, then the distinguishing detail: material, size, colour or model. "Hand-embroidered cotton cushion cover, 18 inch, sage" tells both a shopper and a search engine exactly what is on offer. Keep brand names and clever product nicknames secondary.

The description

  • Open with the benefit in one or two sentences: what problem it solves or what it is for.
  • Follow with specifics in a scannable list: dimensions, materials, care, what is in the box.
  • Answer objections: delivery times, returns, sizing advice.
  • Write it yourself. Manufacturer copy is duplicated across dozens of sites and gives search engines no reason to prefer yours.

Photographs

Show scale, texture and the product in use. Add descriptive alt text to each image. Compress files so the page loads quickly on a phone, which is where most shoppers will see it.

Variants

Where colours or sizes are variants of one product, keep them on one page rather than creating near-identical listings that compete with each other.

Reviews

Genuine reviews help shoppers and can appear in search results through structured data. Never write or buy reviews; it breaches platform and search policies and, in many countries, consumer law. Ask real customers after delivery instead.

The search listing

Edit the SEO title and meta description in Shopify's "Search engine listing" panel. Use the description to state the reason to click: free delivery threshold, handmade, ships in two days, whatever is true and distinctive.

Internal links

Link from the product to its collection and to one or two related items. Link to the product from relevant blog posts and guides. Pages that nothing links to are harder for search engines to find and judge.

What should a product page include, from top to bottom?

  1. Product title. What it is, in the words buyers use, plus the key differentiator: material, size or use.
  2. Photos. Several angles, scale against something familiar, and the product in use.
  3. Price and options. Clear variant selectors, with stock status visible.
  4. Short benefit summary. Two or three lines that say why this one, for whom.
  5. Add to cart. Visible without scrolling on a phone.
  6. Details. Dimensions, materials, care, what is in the box.
  7. Shipping and returns. Costs, times and the returns window, stated near the button.
  8. Reviews. Real ones, including the critical ones.
  9. Related products. Accessories or alternatives that genuinely fit.

How do I write a product description that is not copied?

Manufacturer descriptions appear on every store selling the same item, so they give search engines no reason to prefer yours. Write your own using this sequence:

  • Start with who it is for and the problem it solves.
  • Answer the questions customers ask you in messages and returns. Those are the doubts stopping other people from buying.
  • Give specifics: measurements, weights, materials, compatibility.
  • Be honest about limits. "Not dishwasher safe" prevents returns and builds trust.

Google may rewrite titles and descriptions, so treat these as strong suggestions, not guarantees. Write them for a person deciding which result to click.

How do variants affect SEO?

Keep sizes and colours of the same item as variants on one product, not as separate products with near-identical text. That concentrates reviews and links on one page. Create separate products only when the items are genuinely different and people search for them separately.

What should I do when a product goes out of stock?

  • Temporarily out of stock: keep the page live, show the status and offer a back-in-stock alert.
  • Permanently discontinued: redirect the URL to the closest replacement or its collection.
  • Never leave a popular page returning a 404.

A quick audit for your top products

Open your ten best-selling products on a phone and check: is the price, button and delivery information visible quickly? Is the description your own? Does every image have alt text? Are reviews showing? Fix the gaps on these ten first, as they carry most of the revenue.

Which mistakes cost the most?

  • Leaving the default SEO title, which is often just the product name with no context.
  • Uploading huge, uncompressed photos that slow the page on mobile.
  • Hiding delivery costs until checkout, which drives abandoned carts.
  • Creating a new product for every colour, splitting reviews across pages.
  • Deleting products without redirecting their URLs.
  • Copying the same description across several similar products.

Each of these is quick to fix and worth more than any app or plugin. Work through your best-selling products first, then apply the same pattern to new products as you add them, so the catalogue improves steadily instead of in occasional large clean-ups.

Questions readers ask

How long should a Shopify product description be?

Long enough to answer real buyer questions. A few short paragraphs plus a details list suits most products; thin one-liners and copied text are the bigger problems.

Can I use the manufacturer's product description?

You can, but it duplicates every other store selling the item. An original description written around customer questions gives search engines and buyers a reason to choose you.

Should each colour be a separate product?

Usually no. Keep colours and sizes as variants of one product so reviews and links build up on a single page.

Do reviews help product page SEO?

Genuine reviews add fresh, original text and reassure buyers. With correct structured data, review ratings can also appear in search results, though Google decides when to show them.
