Getting your business mentioned in AI answers: a practical guide

ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews now answer many questions directly. Here is how to be part of the answer.

SmallBizWire Editorial Team · 30 October 2026 · 4 min read

Short answer: To get your business mentioned in AI answers, make it easy for assistants to understand and trust you: publish pages that directly answer customer questions, keep your name, address and services consistent online, maintain a complete Google Business Profile, earn genuine reviews and reputable mentions, use structured data, and let search crawlers access your site.

A growing share of searches end without a click, because an AI-generated answer appears first. Sometimes called generative engine optimisation (GEO) or answer engine optimisation (AEO), the work of appearing in those answers overlaps heavily with good SEO, with a few differences.

Let the crawlers in

AI services use their own crawlers, such as OpenAI's GPTBot and OAI-SearchBot, Anthropic's ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot. Check your robots.txt does not block them if you want to be cited. Some website builders and security services block them by default.

Answer the question directly

AI systems favour passages that answer a question clearly and can be quoted on their own. Put a one- or two-sentence direct answer near the top of the relevant page, then expand. Use the question as a heading where it reads naturally.

Be specific and factual

  • State what you do, where, for whom and at what price range in plain sentences.
  • Use real numbers only when you can source them.
  • Keep facts consistent across your website, Google Business Profile and directory listings.

Use structured data

Organization, LocalBusiness, Product, Article and FAQPage schema help machines understand who you are and what a page says. Validate it with Google's Rich Results Test.

Consider an llms.txt file

llms.txt is a proposed convention: a plain Markdown file at your site root that summarises the site and links to its key pages for language models. Adoption by AI companies is uneven and it is not a ranking factor, but it is cheap to add and harmless.

Be mentioned elsewhere

AI answers draw on what many sources say. Genuine mentions in industry directories, local press, partner sites and review platforms make your business more likely to be named. Earn them honestly; fabricated reviews and paid fake articles carry legal and reputational risk.

Check what the assistants say now

Ask ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Gemini the questions your customers ask. Note whether you appear and what they get wrong, then fix the source pages. Repeat monthly.

How do AI assistants decide which businesses to mention?

No AI company publishes a full formula, so be cautious of anyone who claims to know one. What is generally understood is that many AI answers draw on web search results and on information widely repeated across reputable sources. That means much of the work overlaps with ordinary SEO and local SEO: be findable, be clear, and be talked about consistently.

What are the practical steps?

  1. Answer real questions on your site. Write pages that address the questions customers ask before buying, with the direct answer in the first paragraph.
  2. State the basics plainly. What you do, where, for whom, prices or price ranges, and how to contact you.
  3. Keep details consistent. Your name, address, phone and services should match across your site, Google Business Profile and directories.
  4. Earn mentions. Local press, industry associations, supplier pages and genuine reviews give assistants independent sources to draw on.
  5. Add structured data. Organisation, LocalBusiness, Product and FAQ markup help machines read your pages.
  6. Check crawler access. Make sure your robots.txt does not accidentally block search engines, and decide deliberately which AI crawlers to allow.

How do I check whether AI assistants mention me?

  • Ask several assistants the questions your customers ask, such as "best bakery near [your area] for wedding cakes," and note who is mentioned.
  • Repeat monthly with the same questions so you can see change over time.
  • Check your analytics for referral visits from AI assistant domains.
  • When an assistant gets a fact about you wrong, find where that fact appears online and correct it at the source.

What should I avoid?

  • Stuffing pages with keywords or lists of cities you do not serve.
  • Fake reviews or paid mentions disguised as independent content.
  • Services promising guaranteed placement in AI answers.
  • Thin, mass-produced pages that add nothing new.

Where should a small business start?

Start with the foundations you control. A complete, accurate profile is covered in our guide to Google Business Profile for local SEO. If you sell online, the Shopify SEO guide covers the store side. Then pick the five questions customers ask most and give each a clear, direct answer on your site.

What does a page written for AI answers look like?

Take one question a customer asks, such as "How much does a bathroom refit cost in our area?" Use the question, or something close to it, as a heading. Put a direct, honest answer in the first two or three sentences, including a price range if you can give one. Then explain what changes the answer, list the steps involved, and end with how to contact you. Add the date the page was last reviewed and the name of the business behind it. This format suits human readers who want a quick answer, ordinary search engines, and AI assistants looking for a clear, quotable passage. Repeat it for each of your most common questions, and review the pages whenever prices or services change.

Questions readers ask

What is GEO or AEO?

Generative or answer engine optimisation: making your content easy for AI systems to find, understand and cite in generated answers.

Does llms.txt improve rankings?

It is not a ranking factor and adoption is uneven, but it is a low-cost way to summarise your site for language models.

Can I pay to appear in ChatGPT or other AI answers?

Be wary of anyone selling guaranteed placement. Focus on clear content, consistent details and genuine mentions, which are the things assistants are generally understood to draw on.

Should I block AI crawlers from my site?

It is a business choice. Blocking may protect content but can reduce the chance of being cited. Never block regular search engine crawlers by accident.

How do I fix wrong information an AI gives about my business?

Find where the wrong fact appears online, such as an old directory listing, and correct it at the source. Keep your own site and profiles accurate and consistent.
