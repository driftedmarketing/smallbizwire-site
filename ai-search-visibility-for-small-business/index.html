Getting your business mentioned in AI answers: a practical guide

ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews now answer many questions directly. Here is how to be part of the answer.

SmallBizWire Editors · 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

A growing share of searches end without a click, because an AI-generated answer appears first. Sometimes called generative engine optimisation (GEO) or answer engine optimisation (AEO), the work of appearing in those answers overlaps heavily with good SEO, with a few differences.

Let the crawlers in

AI services use their own crawlers, such as OpenAI's GPTBot and OAI-SearchBot, Anthropic's ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot. Check your robots.txt does not block them if you want to be cited. Some website builders and security services block them by default.

Answer the question directly

AI systems favour passages that answer a question clearly and can be quoted on their own. Put a one- or two-sentence direct answer near the top of the relevant page, then expand. Use the question as a heading where it reads naturally.

Be specific and factual

  • State what you do, where, for whom and at what price range in plain sentences.
  • Use real numbers only when you can source them.
  • Keep facts consistent across your website, Google Business Profile and directory listings.

Use structured data

Organization, LocalBusiness, Product, Article and FAQPage schema help machines understand who you are and what a page says. Validate it with Google's Rich Results Test.

Consider an llms.txt file

llms.txt is a proposed convention: a plain Markdown file at your site root that summarises the site and links to its key pages for language models. Adoption by AI companies is uneven and it is not a ranking factor, but it is cheap to add and harmless.

Be mentioned elsewhere

AI answers draw on what many sources say. Genuine mentions in industry directories, local press, partner sites and review platforms make your business more likely to be named. Earn them honestly; fabricated reviews and paid fake articles carry legal and reputational risk.

Check what the assistants say now

Ask ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Gemini the questions your customers ask. Note whether you appear and what they get wrong, then fix the source pages. Repeat monthly.

Questions readers ask

What is GEO or AEO?

Generative or answer engine optimisation: making your content easy for AI systems to find, understand and cite in generated answers.

Does llms.txt improve rankings?

It is not a ranking factor and adoption is uneven, but it is a low-cost way to summarise your site for language models.
